INLAY

White body, black inlay. Black hides white in two layers, the reverse needs 1.2 mm, which is why the inlay can be this shallow.
Black slivers thinner than min black get dropped, white gaps thinner than min white gap get filled. Both are what the nozzle can actually lay.
mesh quality
12 is 0.083 mm per pixel, well under the nozzle. 16 for lots of small text, 8 for speed.

slicing notes
Print by layer, not by object. 2 walls, 4 top and 4 bottom layers, no supports, elephant foot compensation on. The plate is flow limited so the slicer's time estimate is the real one; the number here is a rough average at 8.5 mm3/s for PETG, 11 for PLA. Back face art is mirrored in the mesh and reads right once flipped; it looks backwards in the slicer's bottom view.

SPRUE.

Batch plates of two colour giveaways. White body, black inlay on both faces, coins and guitar picks.
  1. Blank picks the shape, thickness and inlay depth.
  2. Art drops images or text on each face. Drag on the preview to place it.
  3. Plate sets the count and exports the 3mf with both extruders already assigned.
It opens with a pick that carries "FAN PROJECT 2026 PHX" on the back and an empty front. Drop your art on the front and export.
