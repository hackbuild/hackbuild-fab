BOSS.bench

a LEGO style electronics system you print in four filaments: PETG body, conductive PLA studs and traces, TPU wires and buttons, conductive TPU sockets and cores. Board seats bring ESP32-C3, Pico and Teensy pins out to studs, bricks add buttons, touch, force, sliders, lamps and resistors, printed wires join it all, and the build step is a circuit simulator you snap together in 3D.

PRINT REALITY

Studs 4.8 mm on an 8 mm pitch, LEGO compatible. Sockets are conductive TPU rings at 4.6 mm, so they stretch 0.2 mm over a stud and stay in contact. Everything prints flat with no supports. Conductive PLA (Protopasta class) is about 30 ohm cm in plane; conductive TPU (Eel class) about 1500 ohm cm. That is why every readout shows kilohms: printed conductors carry signals, touch, and dividers. They do not carry motor current or LED strips. Wires with a conductive PLA core are 50x lower than conductive TPU and still bend a little.
Purge into conductive TPU generously (70 mm3 or more) or the black smears the cream. Print the conductive slots at the low end of their temperature range. If the filament list imports as one entry, use the per colour STL zip.
