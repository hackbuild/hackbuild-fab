hack.build OGEE R16 // SHEET PARTS // NEST AND EXPORT DXF 0 PCS 0 SHEETS 0 CM2

sheet

Set your stock and it re-nests. Anything too big for the sheet is called out rather than quietly dropped.

parts

The sheet parts only. Bars, lugs, links, rods, caps, pins, knobs and cranks are all printed and are not here. Click a row to drop it.

export

One DXF per sheet, in millimetres, every part already positioned. Outlines only, no construction geometry, so it goes straight into the cutter.

notes

Cut the coupon first and check the tab fit before committing a sheet. Kerf is not compensated here: the outlines are nominal, and the joints are drawn with 0.15 of clearance on tabs and 0.40 on sliding fits, which suits a kerf around 0.18. If your kerf is much wider, the tabs will be loose rather than tight.

Every outline here is the same geometry the interference audit and the fit checks ran on, pulled from the DXFs rather than redrawn. Inner outlines are cut as well as outer ones, so slots and bores come through.
