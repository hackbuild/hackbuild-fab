PIP.bench

a game controller printed in one go on a four head machine: PETG body, conductive PLA traces buried in it, TPU keys, conductive TPU contacts. a Waveshare RP2040-Zero drops into the pocket.
1 layout

body shape, button groups, flex / clicky / touch per group

2 buttons

cone, snap spring, touch comb and stick geometry with feel readouts

3 board

press fit contacts or wire cups, pin map, wiring and firmware

4 print

stack, routing, filaments, export 3MF, STLs and the print card

FITS AND CLEARANCES

every place two parts meet, with what the printer usually does to it. holes come out about 0.15 mm small and pegs about 0.05 mm big, so a 0.10 mm clearance on paper is a press fit in the hand.

EXPORT
