# Copper surfaces and boards

## The KEYWAY family

Eight designs sharing one method: single copper layer, zero jumpers, zero
crossings, a generator holding every dimension in ONE P dict that REFUSES TO
EMIT unless all gates pass.

| design | size | nets | gates | min clearance | what it explores |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| KEYWAY | 110 x 174 | 8 | 45 | 1.00 | Velostat force pads plus graded slider, 8-layer stack |
| VERNIER | 110 x 214 | 12 | 60 | 1.20 | no Velostat, craft parts only, 6 pads plus 3 wedge sliders |
| QUADRANT | 190 x 134 | 12 | 59 | 1.20 | radial fan, single-row rail with alternating depth |
| SPLINE | 242 x 146 | 18 | 85 | 1.00 | 12 blade keys, radial stub escapes |
| TESSERA | 242 x 176 | 18 | 85 | 1.10 | Voronoi tiles, channels that ARE the routing |
| FRET | 248 x 244 | 21 | 95 | 1.00 | Wicki-Hayden hex, zigzag ribbon routing |
| STAVE | 96 x 250 | 12 | 57 | 1.20 | 8 keys from one slab, fits a 4 inch tape roll |
| COFFER | 108 x 93.5 x5 | 6 each | | | hex tiles that ground-bridge across seams |

The single-row rail replaced staggered rows: N fingers on a fixed pitch with
ALTERNATING DEPTH, so adjacent clip jaws bite several mm apart in y while the
row stays one row.

Honest limits stated in the READMEs: 5 mm side gap plus 5 mm depth offset is
workable with MINI alligator clips and not roomy with full-size ones.

FRET's interval gate is the nicest verification in the family: the generator
WALKS EVERY ADJACENT PAIR and asserts the interval sets are exactly {2}, {7},
{5}. The layout cannot silently stop being isomorphic.

STAVE's gate compares board width against the tape roll width, so raising the
key count or rail pitch FAILS LOUDLY instead of quietly producing something
the roll cannot cut.

Design judgment recorded: SPLINE's thin arced blades and TESSERA's Voronoi
layout were both called dumb and not playable. FRET is the one that is a real
note space. 8 keys equals one octave of ANY scale, so the faceplate is marked
with DEGREES, not note names, and which pitch each degree plays is a firmware
table.

## SPINDLE

Two builds of one design, and the pair is the best worked example of the
copper-tape to PCB path.

V5 vinyl-cut, 54 x 46 mm, down from an earlier 70 x 58. Three prior versions
were rejected outright with "rethink the approach", the core complaint being
repeated near-identical layouts.

The insight that made v5 work: the module's 5V and GND are the TOP TWO PADS of
its right column, so VERTICAL FLANKING HEADERS land VCC and GND straight
across with no fan, and each motor phase becomes one short diagonal with at
most a 2.54 mm rise.

A functional failure worth remembering from v4: the module was buried
mid-board behind a GND ring, making USB physically unreachable. THE USB
CONNECTOR MUST BE AT THE BOARD EDGE.

R1 PCB, 46 x 46 mm, 2 layer, bottom is a GND pour, generated by `pcb.py` which
REFUSES TO WRITE FILES UNLESS DRC PASSES.

The second layer is what unlocks the expansion header: six signals boxed in on
the single-layer version run down the open CHANNEL UNDER THE MODULE, between
the two pad rows, and come out the bottom.

The module footprint is a HYBRID: an oval pad spanning both the edge
castellation AND the 0.6 inch hole row, so the module can be dropped in
through-hole or reflowed on its edge. That is also what caused the drill
position bug.

Final as shipped: 19 nets / 70 pads / 57 holes / 0.32 mm tightest against a
0.25 rule; bottom copper matches 99.91 percent, top 99.28; kiutils round trip
55/55 pads, 0 net mismatches, 0 drill-offset errors, all 19 nets connected.
The legacy DRC printed DRC OK and the README claimed every hole isolated or
bonded, but that DRC missed a short: 5V on B.Cu runs through four GND vias at
y 20.00, so non-GND holes isolated 33 of 35 and GND holes bonded 18 of 22
(computed, D-249). Do not order R1 until the owner picks the fix.

## ARMATURE

100 x 60 mm, 2 layer, 1.6 mm, 2 oz, PINK soldermask with white silk.

The trick that makes three-source power ORing work without a controller: the
boost is trimmed 300 mV UNDER the wired rails, plus a transistor pulling the
boost enable down whenever USB or VIN is present. The charger input is a
SEPARATE NODE from VSYS so the pack can never charge itself.

Deliberately uses no GPIO in 33-37 so N16R8 and N8R2 modules are
interchangeable in the same layout.

STILL OPEN: not routed. Copper and gerbers are the remaining step.

The 3D sim's `check3d.mjs` caught three real design errors the 2D checker
could not, all from KiCad PinHeader footprints running along footprint Y.

## TACK

36 mm round 2-layer. Front is a VPLUS pour with the wrench as a MASK OPENING
(bare ENIG gold) and the arc as black silk. Back carries a CR2032 holder, a
slide switch, and a two-transistor astable at about 0.72 Hz.

The chirality lesson lives here: unmirrored back footprints mean a physically
flipped part lands its pins MIRRORED. Symmetric 2-pad parts do not care;
SOT-23 swaps B and E. The copper was rerouted so the BASE nets land on the
mirrored pin positions.

UNVERIFIED: pink LED Vf is marginal at 3 V (red is the alternate), LCSC
numbers left blank, the switch sim model is a stand-in, front pour rim slots
are cosmetic.

## SHUNT

A shitpost, but the fab-set work in it is real. 154.0 x 110.0 mm, deliberately
0.33 mm short of the real board it parodies, measured from that board's own
Edge_Cuts gerber.

The mirror bug took three attempts and the lesson is in
`kernels/viewer-sim.md`: POSITION MIRRORING AND GLYPH MIRRORING ARE
INDEPENDENT.

The testing lesson is the other half: a probe point SYMMETRIC ABOUT THE BOARD
CENTRELINE is blind to a mirror by construction. Use two features of DIFFERENT
COLOUR at mirror-image x on the same y for position registration, and an
L-shaped fiducial drawn THROUGH the text transform for glyph orientation.

`view_preset.py` perspective-warps the painted texture through the measured
camera quad to produce an actual picture per preset, with no GPU. Added
because two rounds of sign reasoning both gave confident wrong answers.

Gate G7 is INVERTED ON PURPOSE: the build fails unless exactly 5 intentional
shorts and 5 floating nets remain.

OPEN: the fab set was never loaded into a vendor's own viewer, silk is not
pre-clipped off pads, CPL rotations unchecked against an assembler's library,
pad tables not diffed against datasheets.

## COFFER

The port standard is the reusable artifact: identical on all six edges of
every tile, with u mirroring across a seam so A's +14 faces B's -14, and
symmetric tabs so all three mate either way. 214 gates pass across five tiles,
zero jumpers.

The topology rule learned here: THE GROUND RING CAN ONLY BE CUT ONCE PER TILE
without an interior GND feature, because a second cut disconnects it.

D1's dial trick is worth keeping: four radial bands whose widths vary
sinusoidally with angle at 90 degree phase, so absolute angle =
atan2(D2-D4, D1-D3), resolver style, no seam and no dead reckoning. The 55
degree notch where the four traces leave is a real blind sector to mask in
firmware.

M1 (the carrier tile) consequences, not choices: only TWO OPPOSITE EDGES can
carry signals, because the module's two pad rows are a WALL. And 5V rather
than 3V3 takes the under-module channel, because 5V is the pin BELOW GND.
