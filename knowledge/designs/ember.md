# EMBER: the HeatSync flame SAO board

The owner's backlit art board, made with the board-forge skill. The board outline is the HeatSync mark. Nine reverse mount LEDs on the back shine through slots in the flames, and an ATtiny1614 flickers each flame on its own channel. The owner added it on 2026-11-02 as inspiration for hardware work ("something to add for inspo for hardware stuff"). It has not been ordered or built, so it is a source of techniques and lessons, not a bar for parity.

Code root for every location below: `fab-knowledge:originals/ember/files/`. A location written `gen/board.py:346-410` means those lines of that file.

Number qualifiers: `measured` means read here on 2026-11-02 off the archived files (hashes, bytes, line counts, a byte compare); `reported` means the generator's own log or review says so and it was not rerun here; `computed` means arithmetic done here; `vendor` means a part vendor's value as copied into the build; `inferred` and `unknown` mean what they say.

## 3. How it is made

### 3.1 The optical stack is the art

| layer | material | look |
|---|---|---|
| sun | exposed ENIG copper, front | gold, opaque |
| arrow | front copper under white mask | bone white, opaque |
| flames | bare FR4 under white mask, no copper | glows when lit from the back |

This keeps the HeatSync rule that the sun is always a second colour. On a backlit board copper is ink and every back trace is a shadow, so light becomes a set of checks (3.4).

### 3.2 Outline

`geom.py:33-47`: a morphological close of `CLOSE_R = 0.6` mm (`geom.py:16`) so no concave radius is tighter than a router bit, a 0.35 mm grow for a lit rim, floating flame islands joined by `TETHER_W = 1.3` mm tethers (`geom.py:18`) at their nearest points, then a second close; the result is asserted to be one polygon. LED slots are 2.0 x 2.6 mm stadiums on the outline layer, so the fab routes them out.

### 3.3 Placement that knows it must be wired

`geom.py:175-240`, `place_leds`: a greedy coverage search over a 0.5 mm grid places each LED where it lights the most unlit flame area under a linear falloff `GLOW_R` (about 4.6 mm, inferred in the build). A candidate is refused unless its slot sits mostly over flame, both pads clear the edge, each pad has its own free path past the body to the sun disc, a ground via spot lies within 3.5 mm, it keeps a 0.9 mm lane from its neighbours, and it lies tangential to the disc so both pads face the routing area. The LED count fell from 17 to 9 as these rules were added (PLAYBOOK step 10); 9 equals the free pins of the ATtiny1614, one LED per pin.

The layout is planar by construction on the one routable layer: channels are assigned by angle round the sun, resistors sit in two rows in pin order, so pin order, resistor order and LED order are one rotation and no channel crosses another. Ground is the decorative front sun, reached by 11 vias that prefer to hide under the masked arrow.

### 3.4 Gates inside the generator

`gen/board.py:346-410`, `gates`, and the build refuses to write fab files if any fails: routing complete; clearance between every pair of nets (0.20 mm rule, 0.276 mm reported); every signal net one connected piece; every ground piece reaches the front sun by a via or J1.2; every via inside the sun; copper to edge 0.35 mm; copper to slot 0.25 mm; no front copper in the flames; back copper under the flames under 9 percent of the window (6.0 percent reported). The router (`router.py`) is grid A* at 0.1 mm with an 8x cost under flames and 3x on the sun rim, negotiated PathFinder style, then legalised by ripping up only conflicting connections.

### 3.5 Independent verification

`gen/verify.py` re-parses every emitted gerber with pygerber 2.4.3, rasterises it and compares to the source geometry by IoU and a 0.05 mm both-ways test, counts drill hits (17 = 6 header + 11 vias) and checks BOM, CPL and netlist designators are the same set. `fab/verify.txt` reports every layer PASS, IoU 0.866 to 0.997. It hit the same pygerber clear-colour trap `knowledge/kernels/board-pcb.md` records, and a 1 to 2 pixel canvas offset that needed a registration search. Not rerun here.

### 3.6 Firmware from the board

`gen/emit.py:208` writes `fw/ember_channels.h` from the placement: port and bit per channel, which channels are flame tips (slot 2 mm or more from the disc), and the ring order round the sun for the travelling flare. The firmware is 8-bit bit angle modulation at about 490 Hz and a 120 Hz flame model seeded from the chip serial (PLAYBOOK step 20). It was tested on the host against register stand-ins written from memory and never built with avr-gcc (reported).

## 4. Not verified

From `files/REVIEW.md`, kept here so a reader sees it without opening that file:

1. The reverse mount LED land pattern was built from the part family and forum cutouts, not from Lite-On's drawing, which could not be read during the build.
2. The firmware was never compiled for the target or run on silicon.
3. The lit render and the preview glow are a light model, not a measurement; no light coupon was made.
4. Decoupling is one 1 uF, below board-forge's 100 nF per pin plus bulk rule, dropped for space.
5. Costs are a 2026 snapshot.
6. The brief asked for a lit arrow; the arrow does not light, because arrow windows cut the sun into pieces and no layout fit.

Nothing in this record was ordered, printed, flashed or photographed.
