# Trademarks

The hack.build name, the hackbuild name used as a product prefix, and the
hack.build logo files are marks of Moheeb Zara. They sit outside every open
licence in this repository (D-171, owner, default in force).

- The open licences (MIT, MIT-0, CC-BY-4.0, CC-BY-SA-4.0, CERN-OHL-W-2.0)
  grant no right to the marks.
- A fork or a remix renames before it publishes. Its name, logo and package
  names do not use hack.build or a hackbuild prefix.
- Nominative use is allowed: you may say a design is compatible with, or
  derived from, a hack.build design, and link to it.
- The npm package `hackbuild-3mf`, the PyPI package `hackbuild_3mf` and the
  npm scope `@hackbuild` identify the upstream. A fork publishes under
  another name.
- Logo files carry their own path-map row in `tools/licences.json`, ahead of
  the catch-all MIT row: `reference/copper/_inputs/hackbuild-logo.svg` and any
  later logo file.
- Marks held by others (client marks, HeatSync Labs) keep their owners'
  terms (D-147). The project never relicenses them.

## Marks on inlays

An inlay, relief or sticker art layer carries only marks you own or hold a
licence for. Counterfeit branded goods are out of scope (USE.md).

- The skill refuses art that is a mark you do not own, and the canon holds
  no design that carries one.
- A design in this repository that carries a mark held by others names the
  holder and its terms beside the art file.
- Every export's provenance is to record the art source of each inlay layer
  (USE.md, Provenance).

Questions about the marks go to Moheeb Zara <hackbuildvideo@gmail.com>.
